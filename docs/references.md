# References

- *Digital Computer Electronics*, Third Edition, Albert Paul Malvino and Jerald A. Brown. SAP-1 is the architectural basis of this project. The edition is recorded in Ihab's contribution notes; page references have not yet been recorded.
- [Logisim Evolution](https://github.com/logisim-evolution/logisim-evolution), the circuit design and simulation tool. The two saved source versions are 4.1.0 and 5.0.0; see [simulation notes](../simulations/sap-1/README.md).
- [Badreddine's circuit](../simulations/sap-1/badreddine-el-habra/sap1-pc-memory-control.circ) and [Ihab's circuit](../simulations/sap-1/ihab-mahiaoui/Our_8Bit_Computer.circ), the sources for implementation descriptions. Component pages link to versioned Logisim definitions where omitted attributes or port behavior matter.
- Badreddine's manual testing report and Ihab's supplied notes, *SAP-1 Project — Technical Documentation of My Contributions*. Their reported results are preserved in [testing](testing.md). The original reports are not included as separate files.

SAP-1 comes from the textbook; the circuits and signed-display extension are the team's implementation. See [contributors](contributors.md) for the division of work. Project licensing is [undecided](../LICENSE); the referenced textbook and third-party software retain their own licensing.
